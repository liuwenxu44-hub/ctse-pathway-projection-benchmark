## CellBench EPIC correction

The earlier incorrect stage2 responses mixed linear bulk with a log-scale prior.
Those outputs are excluded from the valid benchmark assets and are not counted
as algorithm-failure evidence. The subsequent authorized scale correction reused
four stage1 posteriors and performed four stage2 corrective fits, one per
platform/input arm. No such fit is performed by this release.

Each of these CellBench task histories contains an initial technical-failure
record, an authorized technical-recovery record, and the scale-correction record.
Three task-level records do not mean every stage was fitted three times. The
corrected registry is supplied separately from the historical failed generations.
The declared outer seeds and effective stage2 default seed 123 remain distinct.

Successful termination at the frozen configuration is not a general convergence
claim. The later saved-chain diagnostic collection is a separate bounded design;
it does not retroactively make all fits converged or the whole study blinded.

## What this public test actually establishes

This closes the tested output-to-endpoint route while retaining the source and
stochastic-refit limitations above. It does not certify a complete reconstruction
of every historical worker, a fresh four-method fit, or universal reproducibility.
